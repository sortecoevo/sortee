On 11 November, SORTEE members came together virtually for our 2026 Annual General Meeting (AGM) to celebrate the year's achievements, share updates, and look ahead to what's next.

It has been another busy year for SORTEE! We reached **533 active members across 53 countries**, with nearly half of our current members joining in 2026 – our biggest year yet! We also welcomed two new Local Chapters in Canada and South Asia, hosted seven webinars, and held our fifth annual virtual conference. Across our committees, volunteers have been working hard on publications, appraisal documents, position statements, awards, community activities, and many other initiatives to advance open, reliable and transparent research practices.

## Welcoming our newly elected Board members

We were also pleased to announce the results of our 2026 Board elections. Congratulations to our newly elected members for the 2027–2029 term:

- **Kyle Morrison** – Graduate Student seat
- **Saoirse Kelleher** – Open seat
- **Swastika Issar** – Open seat

A big thank you to all candidates who put themselves forward and to everyone who participated in the election!

## Looking ahead

The AGM also included a presentation of the 2025 Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) survey results by Juliette Tariel-Adam, followed by an open discussion about upcoming initiatives and activities. It was a great opportunity to hear from members, exchange ideas, and think about how we can continue to grow as a community.

## Thank you, SORTEE community!

We want to thank all our members, committee volunteers, Senior Advisory Board members, supporters, and everyone who contributes their time and ideas to SORTEE. None of this would be possible without you!

**Couldn't make it to the AGM?** You can catch up on the [AGM presentation here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1RjTHDwi_xM24cV3KNZqFqxtVB8EuUKKk/view), including updates on membership, finances, and activities across our committees.

Thank you for another fantastic year.
Here's to what comes next!
